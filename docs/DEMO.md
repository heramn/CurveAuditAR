# 90-second live demonstration

Open the verified production URL in a foreground browser. Keep the device
stationary, check system volume and try Replay warning before recording.

**0–15 s:** “Northwest Arkansas's communities are connected by winding roads.
CurveAudit AR helps local agencies inspect road geometry and decide where
a professional field review could be useful.”

**15–35 s:** Open the Auditor. “This pilot processed 1,777 real OpenStreetMap
road ways across four communities. These 1,333 markers are geometry candidates,
not a claim that 1,333 roads are dangerous.” Filter to Madison or Carroll and
click a curve or choose it from the inventory.

**35–50 s:** “Here are the estimated radius and heading change, with a direct
link to the source road. Banking, friction and official speed data are unknown.
That's why we don't label this a safe driving speed.”

**50–65 s:** Click Generate preliminary audit. “The printable report carries
actual selected geometry, missing evidence and a field-review checklist.”
Show Print / Save PDF if time permits, then return.

**65–80 s:** Open Pocket Co-Pilot and immediately press Start demo. “The blue
point follows real road geometry, but movement is simulated. At the threshold,
the browser requests a spoken curve warning.” A 450 m route reaches the
150 m threshold in about 12 seconds.

**80–90 s:** “Next comes field validation with engineers. The goal is
affordable, repeatable infrastructure screening with every assumption visible.”

## Recording checklist

- Verify the live map, dataset and sound before recording.
- Keep source road, radius and report ID visible.
- Hide personal tabs and suppress notifications.
- Capture desktop Auditor and phone-width demo if available.
- State any speech fallback honestly; do not dub unsupported-browser audio
  and imply the application spoke it live.
- Do not claim GPS alerts, validated speeds, measured banking, crash reduction
  or professionally validated priority categories.
